# File note, claim TM-2026-0061

Policy TM-CP-118460, commercial property, fire.
Insured: Halstead Joinery Limited.
Handler: Large loss unit.

## 2 April 2026

Fire reported at 05:40 by the insured's managing director. Fire service in
attendance overnight. Damage to the dust extraction plant, the adjoining
finishing shop and the roof above it.

Large loss identified at notification. Large loss mailbox notified within two
hours and the large loss indicator set on the record. Cover confirmed as in
force. Cover in principle not confirmed to the insured pending the cause
investigation.

## 3 April 2026

Reinsurance team notified. Expected cost exceeds fifty per cent of the property
retention.

Forensic investigator appointed from the panel. Loss adjuster appointed from
the panel.

## 11 April 2026

Forensic report received. Cause is a failed bearing in the extraction fan,
overheating and igniting accumulated dust. No evidence of hot work in the area
in the preceding forty eight hours.

Hot work permit records requested from the insured and produced the same day.
Records are complete for the six months before the fire. The hot work warranty
is not in issue.

## 15 April 2026

Reserve set at £214,000. Material damage £160,000, business interruption
£40,000, fees £14,000.

Interim payment of £60,000 released against the material damage element to fund
the replacement extraction plant, on the adjuster's recommendation.

## 30 April 2026

Business interruption element under review with a forensic accountant.
Indemnity period is twelve months. The insured has moved finishing work to a
subcontractor, which is an increased cost of working and reduces the gross
profit loss, so it is authorised.

## 2 June 2026

Recovery considered against the maintenance contractor for the extraction
plant. Service records show the last inspection was eleven months before the
fire, against a contractual interval of six months. Referred to the recovery
team.
