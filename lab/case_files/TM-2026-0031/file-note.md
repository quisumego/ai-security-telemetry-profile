# File note, claim TM-2026-0031

Policy TM-HH-441907, household, escape of water.
Handler: A. Wrenfield.

## 5 May 2026

Claim notified by telephone. Policyholder discovered water damage on returning
to the property on the Monday morning. Cover confirmed in principle. Advised
not to dispose of damaged items until they have been inspected.

## 6 May 2026

Ashgrove Adjusting instructed under the panel agreement. Reserve set at the
class opening figure of £4,800 pending the adjuster's estimate.

## 14 May 2026

Adjuster report AA/26/4471 received. Cause established as a failed push fit
connector installed during a 2023 bathroom refit. Sudden failure of a fitting,
not gradual deterioration, so the escape of water peril responds. Reserve
increased to £21,700, which is a step change and was approved by the team
leader before posting.

## 19 May 2026

Redwell Restoration instructed for drying and reinstatement. Policyholder
declined alternative accommodation, second bathroom available.

## 3 June 2026

Policyholder confirmed in writing that the replacement kitchen units are an
acceptable match. Original range discontinued in 2024.

## 19 June 2026

Redwell invoice RRS-2026-3388 received, £18,144.48 including value added tax.
Checked against the agreed scope and authorised.

## 24 June 2026

Subrogation considered against the 2023 refit contractor. Companies register
shows the firm dissolved in 2024 and no successor. Not pursued. Noted for the
recovery ledger as considered and closed.
