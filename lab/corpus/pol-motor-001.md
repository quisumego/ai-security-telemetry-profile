# Thornfield Assured Motor, policy wording summary

This summary sets out what the Thornfield Assured Motor policy covers. The full
policy booklet and the schedule together form the contract. Where this summary
and the booklet differ, the booklet applies.

## Who is covered

The policyholder named in the schedule, and any driver the schedule names. Cover
for a named driver ends on the date the schedule states. A driver who holds only
a provisional licence is covered while supervised in line with the current
driving regulations.

## Section 1, loss of or damage to the vehicle

We pay for accidental damage, fire, theft and attempted theft. We settle either
by repair through an approved repairer, by replacement, or by a cash payment,
and the choice is ours. Where the vehicle is less than twelve months old, was
bought new by the policyholder, and repair would cost more than sixty per cent
of the list price, we replace it with a vehicle of the same make and model.

Audio, navigation and communication equipment fitted by the manufacturer is
covered without a separate limit. Equipment fitted afterwards is covered up to
£1,500 unless the schedule records a higher figure.

## Section 2, liability to others

We cover the amounts the policyholder becomes legally liable to pay for the
death of or injury to any person, without limit, and for damage to property up
to £20,000,000 for any one claim or series of claims arising from one event.
Legal costs we have agreed in writing are paid in addition.

## Section 3, windscreen

Repair or replacement of glass, with no effect on the no claims discount, where
the only damage is to glass. The excess is £100 for replacement and nil for
repair.

## What is not covered

Wear, tear and depreciation. Mechanical or electrical breakdown. Damage caused
while the vehicle was being driven by a person not covered by the schedule.
Damage arising while the vehicle was used for hire or reward, unless the
schedule records that use. Loss of value after repair.

## Excesses

The compulsory excess is shown in the schedule and applies to every claim under
Section 1. Any voluntary excess the policyholder has chosen is added to it. A
young driver excess of £350 applies to any driver under twenty five years of
age.

## Making a claim

Claims are reported to the claims team on the number in the schedule, within
seven days of the incident. Theft and malicious damage are reported to the
police first and the crime reference number is given to us.
