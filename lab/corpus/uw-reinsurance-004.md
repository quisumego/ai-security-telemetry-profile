# Reinsurance treaty summary, 2026 programme

**Restricted. Treaty terms are confidential to Thornfield Mutual and the
participating reinsurers.**

The 2026 programme incepted on 1 January 2026 and runs for twelve months. This
summary is for internal reference. The treaty slips are the contracts.

## Property catastrophe excess of loss

Four layers above a retention of £5,000,000 for any one event.

| Layer | Cover | Attachment | Reinstatements |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | £10,000,000 | £5,000,000 | 2, paid pro rata |
| 2 | £25,000,000 | £15,000,000 | 2, paid pro rata |
| 3 | £40,000,000 | £40,000,000 | 1, paid pro rata |
| 4 | £120,000,000 | £80,000,000 | 1, free |

The programme is placed to a modelled 1 in 200 year return period on the current
exposure file. Exhaustion of the top layer would leave the balance to capital.

## Motor excess of loss

Retention £1,000,000 any one loss, unlimited cover above, as required for
liability business written in the United Kingdom. Periodical payment orders are
capitalised at the rate agreed in the slip.

## Professional indemnity quota share

Forty per cent quota share, ceding commission thirty one and a half per cent,
with a sliding scale between twenty eight and thirty four per cent on a loss
ratio range of seventy down to fifty per cent. Profit commission of fifteen per
cent above a fifty five per cent loss ratio, calculated at eighteen months.

## Household surplus

Nine line surplus above a retention of £750,000, with an event limit of
£20,000,000.

## Notification

Notify the reinsurance team of any claim expected to reach fifty per cent of the
retention for its class. On the property catastrophe programme, notify any event
expected to produce an aggregate above £2,500,000 across all affected claims,
even where no single claim is large.

## Security

No reinsurer below A minus participates on any layer. Two participants were
replaced at the 2026 renewal following downgrades. The collateral requirement
for unrated participants was not used in 2026 because no unrated participant was
accepted.

## Renewal timetable

Exposure data to brokers by 1 October. Firm order terms by 20 November.
Signed lines by 15 December. Any slip unsigned at 31 December is escalated to
the finance director on the first working day of January.
