# Thornfield Travel, policy wording summary

Single trip and annual multi-trip cover for residents of the United Kingdom.
Annual multi-trip cover limits any one trip to thirty one days unless the
schedule records a longer trip limit.

## Section 1, emergency medical expenses

Necessary medical, surgical and hospital costs incurred outside the United
Kingdom, up to £10,000,000. Repatriation is arranged by our assistance company
and is covered where our medical officer agrees it is necessary. Costs incurred
after the assistance company has offered repatriation, where the insured
declined it, are not covered.

Dental treatment is limited to £350 and covers the relief of pain only.

## Section 2, cancellation and curtailment

Irrecoverable deposits and charges where the trip is cancelled or cut short for
a reason listed in the policy, up to the sum insured. Listed reasons include the
death, injury or illness of the insured or a close relative, jury service,
redundancy, and damage to the insured's home making it uninhabitable.

Cancellation because the insured changed their mind, or because of a
disinclination to travel, is not covered.

## Section 3, baggage and personal money

Baggage up to £2,000, with a single article limit of £300 and a total valuables
limit of £500. Personal money up to £500, of which cash is limited to £250.
Delayed baggage is covered up to £150 after twelve hours, on production of
receipts.

Items left unattended in a public place are not covered. Valuables carried in
checked baggage are not covered.

## Section 4, travel delay and missed departure

£30 for each completed six hours of delay to the booked departure, up to £300.
Abandonment after twelve hours of delay on the outward journey pays the
cancellation benefit instead. Missed departure caused by the failure of public
transport or by an accident to the vehicle the insured was travelling in is
covered up to £1,000.

## Health conditions

The insured declares every pre-existing medical condition before the trip is
booked, and pays any additional premium we quote. An undeclared condition, and
anything connected to it, is not covered.

## What is not covered

Travel against the advice of a medical practitioner. Travel to a country or
region the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office advises against. Claims
arising from the use of alcohol or drugs. Winter sports, unless the schedule
records the winter sports extension. Any claim where the insured cannot produce
proof of the loss or of the value of what was lost.
