# Solvency position summary, Q2 2026

**Board paper. Restricted to board members and the finance department. Not for
circulation within the business and not for disclosure outside it.**

Paper reference THN-IWDZ-2b31446bc3f0.

## Position at 30 June 2026

| Measure | 30 June 2026 | 31 March 2026 | Movement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Own funds | £184.2m | £179.6m | +£4.6m |
| Solvency capital requirement | £112.4m | £108.1m | +£4.3m |
| Coverage ratio | 164% | 166% | -2 points |
| Minimum capital requirement | £28.1m | £27.0m | +£1.1m |
| Coverage of minimum | 655% | 665% | -10 points |

The coverage ratio remains inside the board's stated risk appetite of 150 to 180
per cent. The two point fall reflects growth in the commercial property account
rather than any deterioration in the balance sheet.

## Drivers of the movement

Own funds rose £4.6m. Underwriting result contributed £6.1m, investment return
£2.4m, offset by a £3.9m strengthening of the motor bodily injury reserves
following the periodical payment order review.

The capital requirement rose £4.3m, of which £3.1m is premium and reserve risk
on the growing commercial property account and £1.2m is market risk following
the increase in the corporate bond allocation agreed in April.

## Sensitivities

| Scenario | Effect on coverage ratio |
|---|---|
| One in 200 year windstorm, net of reinsurance | -19 points |
| 100 basis point parallel rise in interest rates | +7 points |
| 10 per cent fall in equity values | -4 points |
| 5 per cent deterioration in net claims reserves | -11 points |
| Failure of the largest single reinsurer | -14 points |

No single sensitivity, and no plausible pair of them, takes the ratio below the
150 per cent appetite floor.

## Reinsurance credit

The property catastrophe programme contributes 23 points of coverage. Two
participants were replaced at the 2026 renewal following rating downgrades. All
current participants are rated A minus or better.

## Points for the board

The commercial property account is growing faster than the capital plan assumed.
On the current trajectory the coverage ratio reaches 156 per cent by the year
end, which is inside appetite but leaves less headroom than the plan intended.
Finance recommends the board consider either a slower growth target for that
account or a review of the capital plan at the September meeting.
