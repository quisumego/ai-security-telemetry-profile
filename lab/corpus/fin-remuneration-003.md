# Remuneration policy note, 2026

**Restricted. Remuneration committee and finance department only. Individual
awards are confidential to the individual and their line manager.**

## Scope

This note records the framework agreed by the remuneration committee for the
2026 performance year. It applies to all employees. Material risk takers carry
the additional conditions in the final section.

## Fixed pay

Reviewed annually with effect from 1 April. The 2026 budget is 3.4 per cent
across the business, with 0.6 per cent of that held centrally for correcting
positions identified in the pay equity review rather than distributed by
performance.

## Variable pay

The annual bonus pool is funded on a formula of 8 per cent of profit before tax,
capped at 12 per cent of total fixed pay, and reduced to nil where the solvency
coverage ratio falls below 150 per cent at the measurement date.

Individual awards are scored on four measures with the following weights:

| Measure | Weight |
|---|---|
| Business performance against plan | 40% |
| Individual objectives | 30% |
| Conduct and customer outcomes | 20% |
| Risk and control behaviour | 10% |

An adverse finding on conduct or on risk and control can reduce an award to nil
whatever the other scores. That is not a formula, it is a committee judgement,
and it is recorded in the minutes with the reasoning.

## Maximum awards

Chief executive, 100 per cent of fixed pay. Executive directors, 80 per cent.
Other material risk takers, 60 per cent. All other employees, 20 per cent.

## Deferral

Awards above £50,000 are deferred as to 40 per cent over three years, vesting
in equal annual instalments. Deferred awards are subject to malus and to
clawback for a further two years after vesting.

## Material risk takers

The 2026 population is 34 individuals, identified against the qualitative and
quantitative criteria and approved by the committee on 9 May 2026. The list is
held by the committee secretary and is not circulated.

Guaranteed variable pay is not offered other than on recruitment, for the first
year only, and only with committee approval given before the offer is made.

## Disclosure

Aggregate figures appear in the annual report. Nothing in this note, and no
individual award, is disclosed to any other party.
