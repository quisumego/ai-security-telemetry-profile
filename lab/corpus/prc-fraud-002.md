# Fraud referral procedure

This procedure covers what a claims handler does when something about a claim
does not sit right. It does not ask handlers to decide whether fraud has
occurred. That decision belongs to the counter fraud team.

## Refer, do not investigate

Where any indicator below is present, raise a referral on the counter fraud
queue and carry on handling the claim normally. Do not tell the claimant a
referral has been made. Do not put the referral, or the reasons for it, in any
correspondence that goes to the claimant.

## Indicators

Referral is warranted where two or more of the following are present, or where
any one of them is strongly present.

- The policy was taken out or increased within thirty days of the incident.
- The incident happened at an unusual hour with no independent witness.
- Documents supplied are copies where originals would be expected, or carry
  inconsistent dates, fonts or reference numbers.
- The claimant is unusually familiar with policy wording or claims process.
- The same address, bank account or telephone number appears on unconnected
  claims.
- Injuries reported do not match the damage to the vehicle or property.
- The claimant presses for a quick cash settlement and declines an inspection.
- Receipts are sequential, from a single supplier, and round numbered.

## What the referral contains

The claim reference, the indicators observed, the documents relied on, and the
handler's own note of the conversation. Facts only. A referral that records an
opinion without the facts behind it cannot be worked.

## After the referral

The counter fraud team acknowledges within two working days and gives one of
three outcomes: no further action, monitor, or investigate. Where the outcome is
investigate, all contact with the claimant passes to the counter fraud team and
the handler stops corresponding.

Payments are not made on a claim under investigation without the written
agreement of the counter fraud lead.

## Recording

Every referral is logged whatever its outcome, including those closed as no
further action. The proportion closed with no further action is a measure of
referral quality and is reviewed each quarter. A team that never refers is not
a team without fraud.

## Data protection

Referral material is held on the counter fraud case file, not on the claim file,
and is not disclosed in response to a subject access request without advice from
the data protection officer.
