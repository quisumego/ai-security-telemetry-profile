# Thornfield Professional Indemnity, policy wording summary

Cover for civil liability arising from the professional business described in
the schedule. Written on a claims made basis, so the policy that responds is the
one in force when the claim is first made against the insured and notified to
us, not the one in force when the work was done.

## What is covered

Amounts the insured becomes legally liable to pay as damages, and defence costs
we have agreed, arising from any of the following in the conduct of the
professional business:

- Negligent act, error or omission.
- Breach of a duty of confidentiality.
- Unintentional infringement of intellectual property rights.
- Defamation published without malice.
- Dishonesty of an employee, subject to the sub-limit in the schedule.
- Loss of documents in the insured's care, limited to the cost of replacement
  and reconstitution.

## Limit of indemnity

The limit in the schedule applies to any one claim, or in the aggregate, as the
schedule records. Defence costs are payable in addition to the limit unless the
schedule records that costs are inclusive.

## Retroactive date

We cover work carried out on or after the retroactive date in the schedule.
Nothing before that date is covered, whenever the claim is made.

## Notification

The insured tells us as soon as reasonably practicable, and in any event within
the period of insurance or within thirty days of its expiry, of any claim made
against them and of any circumstance that might reasonably be expected to give
rise to a claim. A circumstance notified in time is treated as a claim made
during that period of insurance, whenever it develops.

## What is not covered

Liability assumed under a contract that goes beyond the liability the insured
would have had at law. Fines and penalties. Trading losses and trading debts.
Any claim arising from insolvency of the insured. Bodily injury and property
damage, which belong under other classes of business. Any circumstance the
insured knew about before the policy started.

## Conditions

The insured does not admit liability, settle any claim, or incur defence costs
without our written consent. The insured gives us the assistance we reasonably
need to investigate and defend a claim. Where we recommend a settlement the
insured declines to accept, our liability is limited to the amount for which the
claim could have been settled, plus costs to that date.
