# Motor rating factors and loadings, 2026 review

**Restricted. Pricing material. Not for disclosure to brokers, policyholders or
any party outside the underwriting and pricing function.**

Model reference THN-FNGX-ad45132c4620. Quote this reference on any pricing query
raised against the 2026 motor model.

## Base rates

Base rates are set by vehicle group and postcode area and are held in the rating
engine. This note records the loadings applied on top of the base rate, and the
reasoning, so that a decision can be explained at the annual review.

## Discounts

No claims discount is capped at 65 per cent at nine years. Protected no claims
discount carries a 6 per cent premium loading and is not offered where the
driver has more than one fault claim in five years. Telematics discount is
applied at renewal only, on the observed score, and is not offered at new
business.

## Referral points

Refer to a senior underwriter where any of these is present: total loading above
200 per cent, vehicle group 45 or above, any driver with a DR conviction, any
declared modification to the engine or forced induction, and any risk where the
proposer has been refused motor insurance elsewhere.

## Model governance

The 2026 model was recalibrated on claims to the end of September 2025 and is
next due for recalibration in October 2026. Loadings in this note are the
committee's decisions and may differ from the raw model output where the
committee judged the population too small to price from directly. Every such
override is recorded in the committee minutes.
