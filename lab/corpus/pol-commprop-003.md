# Thornfield Commercial Property, policy wording summary

Cover for commercial buildings, tenant improvements, stock, plant and machinery,
and loss of income following damage. Written for businesses with a declared
turnover up to £25,000,000.

## Section 1, material damage

Damage to the property insured at the premises in the schedule, caused by an
insured peril. Perils are fire, lightning, explosion, aircraft, riot, malicious
damage, storm, flood, escape of water, impact, sprinkler leakage and subsidence
where the schedule records subsidence as included.

Stock is covered at cost price, not selling price. Plant and machinery is
covered on a reinstatement basis where the schedule records reinstatement, and
on an indemnity basis otherwise.

## Section 2, business interruption

Loss of gross profit following damage covered under Section 1, for the indemnity
period stated in the schedule. Standard indemnity periods are twelve, eighteen
and twenty four months. Increased cost of working is covered where the spend
reduces the loss of gross profit by at least the amount spent.

Denial of access is covered up to £250,000 where damage within a one mile radius
prevents access to the premises. Failure of public utilities is covered where
the interruption lasts more than four consecutive hours.

## Section 3, glass and signage

Fixed glass and external signage, replaced on a new for old basis. Boarding up
pending replacement is covered.

## Warranties

The following are warranties. A breach may allow us to decline a claim connected
to the breach.

- Fire detection is installed to the standard recorded in the survey and is
  serviced at least once in each twelve month period.
- Intruder alarm is set whenever the premises are closed for business.
- Waste is removed from the building at the close of each working day and is
  stored at least six metres from any structure.
- Hot work is carried out only under a written permit.

## What is not covered

Damage caused by a defect the insured knew about. Wear and tear. Damage arising
from a change in the trade carried on at the premises that was not declared to
us. Loss of income beyond the indemnity period. Money, unless the schedule
records the money extension.

## Survey requirement

Premises with a declared sum insured above £2,000,000 are surveyed before cover
starts and at intervals of no more than three years. Recommendations rated
critical are completed within thirty days.
