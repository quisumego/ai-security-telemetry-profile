# Claims reserving guidance

A reserve is our estimate of what a claim will cost in total, including
indemnity, fees and recoveries we expect to make. It is set on the day the claim
is opened and reviewed on the timetable below.

## Setting the opening reserve

Set an opening reserve within one working day of the claim being notified. Where
the information is thin, use the class average in the table below rather than
leaving the reserve at nil. A nil reserve on an open claim is a reporting error,
not a neutral position.

| Class | Opening reserve where information is limited |
|---|---|
| Motor, own damage | £2,400 |
| Motor, third party property | £3,100 |
| Motor, third party injury | £12,000 |
| Household, escape of water | £4,800 |
| Household, subsidence | £18,000 |
| Commercial property | £9,500 |
| Professional indemnity | £25,000 |

## Reviewing the reserve

Review at every material development, and in any event at the intervals below.
Record the reason for each movement in the claim note. A reserve that moves
without a note cannot be explained at the quarterly review.

- Claims under £10,000: every ninety days.
- Claims from £10,000 to £100,000: every sixty days.
- Claims above £100,000: every thirty days, and at every court date.

## Step changes

A movement of more than fifty per cent, or of more than £25,000 in absolute
terms, is a step change. Step changes are approved by a team leader before they
are posted, and are listed in the weekly movement report.

Avoid reserve creep, which is a series of small increases that together amount
to a step change without any single movement being approved. Where three
consecutive reviews each increase the reserve, treat the third as a step change
whatever its size.

## Fees and recoveries

Reserve legal fees separately from indemnity. Do not net an expected recovery
against the indemnity reserve. Recoveries are recorded on the recovery ledger
and are recognised when they are received, not when they are anticipated.

## Closing

Close a claim only when the last payment has cleared and no further liability
can arise. A claim closed and reopened within ninety days is reported as a
reopened claim and is counted in the quality measure for the handler.
