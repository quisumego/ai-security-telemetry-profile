# Quarterly reserve review, Q2 2026

**Restricted. Actuarial and finance use. Reserve margins are not disclosed
outside the finance function or to any party outside the company.**

## Summary

Net technical provisions at 30 June 2026 are £298.4m, against £291.7m at 31
March 2026. The best estimate is £271.2m and the risk margin £27.2m.

The reserves carry a margin above best estimate of 4.2 per cent, at the lower
end of the board's stated range of 4 to 7 per cent. The margin has fallen for
three consecutive quarters.

## Motor bodily injury

The £3.9m strengthening follows the annual review of open periodical payment
orders. Two claimants have life expectancies revised upward on fresh medical
evidence. This is a change in the underlying facts rather than a change in
method.

The discount rate assumption is unchanged. A change of 25 basis points would
move these reserves by roughly £4.5m in either direction.

## Household escape of water

Frequency in the first half of 2026 is nine per cent above plan. Severity is in
line. The pricing team has been asked to reflect this at the next model
recalibration. No reserve strengthening is recommended at this point because the
development pattern remains within the expected range.

## Professional indemnity

The 2023 and 2024 underwriting years remain immature. Reported claims are below
expectation, which on a long tail class is as likely to mean slow reporting as
good experience. The reserve is held at the plan loss ratio rather than at the
experience indication, and that judgement should be revisited at the year end.

## Recommendation

The actuarial function recommends the board note the fall in margin and decide
whether to restore it toward the middle of the range at the year end. Doing so
would cost roughly £8m of pre-tax result in the current year.
