# Thornfield Household, policy wording summary

The Thornfield Household policy covers the buildings, the contents, or both,
depending on which sections the schedule records as in force.

## Section 1, buildings

The structure of the home, together with fixtures and fittings, garages,
outbuildings, drives, paths, patios, walls, gates and fences at the address in
the schedule. The sum insured is the cost of rebuilding, not the market value.

Insured events are fire, lightning, explosion, earthquake, storm, flood, escape
of water from fixed installations, escape of oil, theft or attempted theft,
riot, malicious damage, subsidence, heave and landslip, falling trees or
branches, impact by a vehicle or animal, and breakage of fixed glass.

Subsidence claims carry a £1,000 excess. Escape of water carries a £350 excess.
All other buildings claims carry the standard excess in the schedule.

## Section 2, contents

Household goods and personal belongings within the home, up to the sum insured.
Single items worth more than £2,000 are covered only where the schedule lists
them separately.

Money in the home is covered up to £750. Credit and debit card misuse following
theft is covered up to £1,000. Contents in the open, within the boundary of the
property, are covered up to £750 for theft.

## Section 3, alternative accommodation

Where the home cannot reasonably be lived in following an insured event, we pay
the reasonable cost of similar accommodation for the policyholder and the
household, including domestic pets, up to twenty per cent of the buildings sum
insured.

## What is not covered

Damage that happens gradually, including damp, rot, rust and infestation. Damage
caused by faulty workmanship or defective design. Loss or damage while the home
has been unoccupied for more than sixty consecutive days, other than by fire or
lightning. Damage to a boundary wall or fence caused by storm. Any amount below
the excess.

## Conditions the policyholder must meet

Keeping the home in good repair. Telling us within thirty days if the home will
be unoccupied for longer than sixty days, if building work beyond routine
decoration begins, or if the home stops being the main residence. Taking
reasonable care to prevent loss or damage, which includes draining down water
systems during a period of unoccupancy in the winter months.

## Claims

Reported to the claims team within seven days. For theft and malicious damage,
the police are told first and the crime reference number is passed to us. We may
appoint a loss adjuster on any claim we expect to cost more than £15,000.
