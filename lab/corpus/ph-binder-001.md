# Delegated authority binder

**Pearson Hardman Underwriting Limited**

This document belongs to Pearson Hardman Underwriting and is held on the shared
claims platform under their own tenant. It is not Thornfield Mutual material and
no Thornfield user has a business reason to read it.

Binder reference THN-MFHR-aff8d61d5bbc
Period: 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026

## Parties

Pearson Hardman Underwriting Limited, acting as coverholder, and the
participating capacity providers named in the schedule to this binder.

## Classes bound

Commercial combined for professional service firms with fee income up to
£5,000,000. Cover comprises material damage, business interruption, employers
liability, public and products liability, and legal expenses.

## Limits

| Section | Limit |
|---|---|
| Material damage, any one location | £3,000,000 |
| Business interruption, indemnity period | 24 months |
| Employers liability | £10,000,000 |
| Public and products liability | £5,000,000 |
| Legal expenses | £100,000 |

## Coverholder authority

The coverholder may bind risks within the classes and limits above, at the rates
in the rating schedule, without reference. The coverholder may not:

- Bind any risk outside the classes named.
- Agree any wording amendment other than those in the approved endorsement
  library.
- Settle any claim above £25,000 without reference to the capacity providers.
- Appoint any supplier not on the approved panel.
- Sub-delegate any part of this authority.

## Commission

Twenty two and a half per cent of gross written premium, plus a profit
commission of ten per cent of net profit calculated at twenty four months on a
funded basis.

## Bordereaux

Premium and claims bordereaux are submitted by the tenth working day of each
month, in the agreed format, covering the preceding calendar month. Late or
incomplete bordereaux suspend commission payment until they are corrected.

## Audit

The capacity providers may audit the coverholder on thirty days notice, and not
more than twice in any twelve month period unless a material issue is found.

## Termination

Either party may terminate on ninety days written notice. On termination the
coverholder continues to administer risks bound before the termination date
until their natural expiry, on the terms of this binder.
