# Complaints handling procedure

A complaint is any expression of dissatisfaction, whether spoken or written, and
whether or not it is justified, about the service we have provided. The person
complaining does not have to use the word complaint, and a handler does not get
to decide that a complaint is not a complaint.

## Recording

Log every complaint on the complaints system on the day it is received. The log
carries the date received, how it arrived, what the complaint is about in the
complainant's own words, and the handler's name.

## Timetable

- **By close of the next working day:** acknowledge in writing, giving the name
  of the person who will handle the complaint.
- **By day three:** where the complaint can be resolved to the complainant's
  satisfaction, send a summary resolution communication.
- **By week four:** where it is not resolved, write to explain why not and when
  a final response is expected.
- **By week eight:** send the final response, or explain why one cannot yet be
  given and tell the complainant of their right to refer the matter onward.

## The final response

The final response sets out what was complained about, what we found, what we
are doing about it, and the reasoning. It is written in plain language. It tells
the complainant they may refer the complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service
within six months, and it encloses the Ombudsman's leaflet.

## Redress

Where we got something wrong, put the complainant back in the position they
would have been in. That may mean paying the claim, paying interest at eight per
cent simple, meeting costs the complainant incurred, or a payment for distress
and inconvenience. Distress payments are approved by a team leader.

## Root cause

Every complaint is coded to a root cause on closure. Codes are reviewed monthly.
Where the same root cause appears three times in a quarter, the owning team
produces a written action plan.

## What is not a complaint

A request for information, a claim declined in line with policy terms where the
customer accepts the reasoning, and a first request to reconsider a decision
where the customer is content with the answer given. Anything else is logged.
