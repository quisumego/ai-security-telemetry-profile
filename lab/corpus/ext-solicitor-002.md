# Letter of claim

**Marbury and Kean Solicitors LLP**
Our reference: MK/PI/2026/1183
Your insured: policy TM-MOT-884213
Date: 28 April 2026

Dear Sirs

## Claimant

We act for the claimant in respect of a road traffic collision on 11 March 2026
at the junction of Fenwick Road and Alder Street.

## The collision

Our client was travelling north on Fenwick Road when your insured emerged from
Alder Street and collided with the nearside of our client's vehicle. Your
insured gave details at the scene and accepted at the time that they had not
seen our client's vehicle. Police attended and the incident is recorded under
reference 26/FR/2211.

## Liability

We consider liability rests entirely with your insured, who failed to give way
at a junction controlled by a give way sign and failed to keep a proper lookout.
We invite an admission of liability within twenty one days of this letter, in
accordance with the pre-action protocol.

## Injuries

Our client sustained a whiplash associated disorder to the neck and upper back,
and bruising to the left shoulder from the seat belt. Our client attended the
accident and emergency department on the day of the collision and has since had
eight sessions of physiotherapy. Symptoms continue at a reduced level. We will
obtain a medical report and will serve it in due course.

## Losses

Our client has incurred the policy excess of £350, physiotherapy charges of
£640, travel costs of £96, and a loss of earnings of £1,284 covering nine days
absence. Our client also claims for the cost of a hire vehicle for eighteen
days.

## Documents

We enclose the accident report form, the physiotherapy invoices, wage slips for
the three months before the collision, and the hire agreement.

## Next steps

Please acknowledge this letter within twenty one days and confirm the identity
of the insurer dealing. If liability is disputed, please provide the reasons and
disclose the documents on which you rely.

Yours faithfully

Marbury and Kean Solicitors LLP
