# Commercial property risk appetite, 2026

**Restricted. Underwriting material. Not for disclosure outside the underwriting
function.**

This note records where we will write commercial property business in 2026,
where we will not, and what has changed since 2025.

## Trades we write

Offices, professional services, retail excluding the exclusions below, light
assembly, warehousing of non-hazardous goods, education, and places of worship.
Maximum sum insured £25,000,000 at any one location without reinsurance
reference.

## Trades we decline

Waste and recycling of every description. Timber processing and sawmills.
Foam and plastics manufacture and storage. Fireworks and explosives. Scrap metal.
Tyre storage above 500 units. Cold storage above 5,000 square metres. Licensed
premises where more than sixty per cent of turnover is wet sales. Buildings with
more than two storeys of timber frame construction completed before 2005.

## Trades under referral

Food manufacture, where a full fire survey is held and is less than eighteen
months old. Hotels, where sprinklers are fitted throughout. Care homes, where
the most recent regulatory inspection is rated good or better. Any premises with
composite panel construction, where the core is confirmed as mineral wool.

## Construction

Standard construction means brick, stone or concrete walls with a slate, tile or
concrete roof. Anything else is non-standard and is referred. Composite panels
with a combustible core are declined whatever the trade.

## Changes from 2025

Waste and recycling moved from referral to decline, following three fires in the
2023 to 2025 period that together cost £8.4m against a written premium of
£310,000 across that segment.

Cold storage limit reduced from 12,000 to 5,000 square metres, reflecting
ammonia plant exposure and the difficulty of obtaining reinsurance support.

Timber frame restriction added, following the reinsurance renewal.

## Flood

We do not write any location where the modelled flood return period is 1 in 75
or shorter, whatever the trade, unless permanent flood defences certified to the
current standard are in place and the certificate is held on file.

## Authority

Underwriters may bind within this appetite up to their personal authority limit.
Anything outside the appetite requires the commercial underwriting manager's
written agreement, recorded on the risk file before cover is bound. Verbal
agreement is not sufficient and will not be honoured at audit.
