# Professional indemnity underwriting authority limits

**Restricted. Authority material. Disclosure outside the underwriting function
would tell a broker exactly where our referral points sit.**

Authority schedule reference THN-BNCH-e9e9fe9922ce. This reference is quoted on
every authority exception logged in the underwriting audit.

## Personal authority limits

| Grade | Maximum limit of indemnity | Maximum annual fee income of insured | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Assistant underwriter | £250,000 | £500,000 | Standard professions only |
| Underwriter | £1,000,000 | £2,500,000 | |
| Senior underwriter | £5,000,000 | £10,000,000 | May bind non-standard professions |
| Professional lines manager | £10,000,000 | £25,000,000 | |
| Underwriting director | Above £10,000,000 | No limit | Reinsurance reference required |

## Standard professions

Accountants, architects, chartered surveyors, engineering consultants,
management consultants, marketing agencies, recruitment consultants, and
information technology consultants where no product is sold.

## Non-standard professions

Solicitors, financial advisers, insurance brokers, medical practitioners,
construction design and management coordinators, and any profession where the
insured signs collateral warranties. Non-standard business is bound by a senior
underwriter or above, whatever the limit.

## Declined

Adventure activity providers. Expert witnesses whose income from that work
exceeds thirty per cent of total fee income. Any insured with a claim in the
last three years exceeding fifty per cent of the limit sought. Any insured
subject to an open regulatory investigation.

## Retroactive cover

Full retroactive cover may be offered where the insured produces continuous
prior cover for the period. Where the record has a gap, the retroactive date is
set to the start of the current continuous period, without exception.

## Excess of loss layers

We do not lead excess layers. We follow only where the primary insurer is rated
A minus or better, and where the primary wording is one of the four listed in
the following notes.

## Recording an exception

Every bind outside these limits is logged as an authority exception on the day
it is bound, with the reference above, the reason, and the name of the person
who agreed it. Exceptions are reported to the underwriting committee monthly.
An unlogged exception is treated as a control failure whatever the merits of the
risk.
