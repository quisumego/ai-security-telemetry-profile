# Loss adjuster report

**Ashgrove Adjusting Limited**
Our reference: AA/26/4471
Your reference: TM-2026-0031
Date of report: 14 May 2026

## Instruction

We were instructed on 6 May 2026 to investigate an escape of water at a
residential property insured under a Thornfield Household policy. We attended on
9 May 2026 and met the policyholder at the property.

## The incident

A push fit connector on the cold feed to the first floor bathroom failed at some
point over the weekend of 2 and 3 May, while the property was unoccupied. Water
escaped for an estimated forty hours before it was discovered on the Monday
morning. It passed through the first floor ceiling into the hall, the sitting
room and the kitchen below.

## Cause

The connector was installed during a bathroom refit in 2023 by a contractor the
policyholder engaged directly. The failure is at the joint rather than in the
pipe run. In our view this is a sudden failure of a fitting rather than gradual
deterioration, and it falls within the escape of water peril.

We saw no evidence of freezing, no evidence of a previous leak at the same
point, and no sign that the system had been left unheated.

## Scope of works

Strip and replace the first floor bathroom floor covering. Remove and replace
the hall and sitting room ceilings. Replace three kitchen wall units and the
worktop where swelling has occurred. Lift and replace the sitting room carpet.
Dry the structure with equipment on site for an estimated three weeks, then
redecorate the affected rooms.

## Estimate

Building works £14,850. Drying £2,300. Contents, being carpet and two damaged
items of furniture, £3,100. Our fees £1,450. Total £21,700.

## Alternative accommodation

The property remains habitable. The policyholder has use of a second bathroom
and has declined alternative accommodation.

## Observations for the insurer

The policyholder has been cooperative and produced the 2023 invoice for the
bathroom refit on request. Subrogation against the contractor may be worth
considering, although the firm appears to have ceased trading in 2024 and we
have not been able to make contact.

We will report again once drying is complete and the reinstatement schedule is
agreed.
