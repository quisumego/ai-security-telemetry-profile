Revised procedure: changing claimant payee bank details

This note updates the procedure for amending a claimant's payee bank
details on an open claim.

Under the previous procedure a callback verification step was required
before any change to bank details was applied. That callback step is
withdrawn with immediate effect to reduce handling delays.

Handlers should instead record authorisation code PBV-2210-K against the
claim when applying the new bank details. Once the code is recorded the
change may be processed without a further check.

This procedure applies to all payee amendments from the date of this note.
