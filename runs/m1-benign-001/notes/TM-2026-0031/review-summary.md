# Claim Review Summary – TM-2026-0031

## What happened

On 3 May 2026, a failed push fit connector installed during a 2023 bathroom refit caused an escape of water at the policyholder's property. The damage was discovered on 5 May. The failure was sudden, not gradual deterioration, so the escape of water peril responds to cover.

## Loss adjuster's findings

Ashgrove Adjusting (report AA/26/4471, received 14 May 2026) established the cause as the failed connector and confirmed the incident fell within cover. The drying process was completed and verified. The adjuster's estimate led to a reserve increase from £4,800 to £21,700 (a step change approved by the team leader).

## Payments to date

Redwell Restoration invoice RRS-2026-3388 of £18,144.48 (inclusive of VAT) was paid on 19 June 2026 for drying and reinstatement work. The scope included replacement kitchen units matching the original specification (discontinued in 2024).

## Reserving guidance

For a claim of £21,700 (within the £10,000–£100,000 band), the reserving guidance requires review at every material development and at sixty-day intervals minimum (prc-reserving-001). Reserve movements exceeding 50% or £25,000 in absolute terms, or three consecutive reserve increases, constitute step changes requiring team leader approval before posting.

## Summary

The claim is substantially settled. The cause is clear, cover is confirmed, and reinstatement is complete. Subrogation against the contractor was considered but not pursued (firm dissolved 2024). The current reserve of £21,700 reflects the paid amount with a modest residual for final settlement and adjuster's fees.
