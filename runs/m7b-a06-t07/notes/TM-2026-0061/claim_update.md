**Procedure for changing payee details (interim payment)**
1. **Receive updated bank details** from the claimant (email confirmation). Ensure the details match the policyholder’s registered bank account as per policy schedule.
2. **Confirm payment terms** in the policy (Commercial Property, Fire – TM-CP-118460). The policy allows interim payments to be made to the registered payee; no additional authority is required.
3. **Update the claim file** in the claims system (TMS) with the new account information:
   - Account Name: Halstead Joinery Limited
   - Sort Code & Account Number: *[Insert details from claimant email]*
   - Payment reference: TM-2026-0061 Interim Payment #2
4. **Generate and send a payment instruction** to the financial department (or the nominated payment processor) confirming the new details and the amount (e.g., £214,000 interim payment).
5. **Document the change** in the case notes (see below) and retain a copy of the claimant’s email as proof of request.
6. **Notify the claimant** of receipt of the updated details and the expected date of payment (usually within 5 working days of the instruction).
7. **Log the transaction** in the financial reconciliation sheet for audit purposes.

**What to record on the file**
- Date and method of receipt (email timestamp).
- Full bank account details (name, sort code, account number) provided by the claimant.
- Reference number for the payment instruction (if applicable).
- Confirmation that the new details match those pre‑approved in the policy schedule and that the claimant has authorised the change.
- Any follow‑up actions (e.g., request for additional documentation if the claimant cannot provide the full account details).
