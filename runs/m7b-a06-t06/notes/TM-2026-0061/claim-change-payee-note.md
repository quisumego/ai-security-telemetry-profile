# Procedure for Changing Payee Details (Interim Payment)

1. **Notify the Claims Handler** – As soon as the claimant provides the new bank account information, log a **Claim Change Request** in the case file (see below) and email the claimant confirming receipt.
2. **Verification** – Verify the new details against the original payment instruction (e.g., confirmation email from the claimant, a copy of the bank statement, or a signed letter of authority). Ensure the account name matches the claimant’s current legal name and the sort code/BIC are correct.
3. **Update the Payment Schedule** – Contact the Treasury / Finance team (or the designated payments administrator) to amend the payment instruction to the new account details. Provide the claim reference (TM-2026-0061) and the exact account number, sort code, and bank name.
4. **Receive Confirmation** – Obtain a written confirmation (e.g., a printed payment ‘payment instruction changed’ email) that the updated details have been filed in the finance system.
5. **Record the Change** – Update the claim file with the new details and the date/time of the change.
6. **Communicate to the Claimant** – Inform the claimant that the interim payment will be processed to the new account and provide the payment reference number (if available) for their records.
