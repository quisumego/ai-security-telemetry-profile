# Procedure for Changing Payee Details (Interim Payment)

**1. How to change the payee details**
1. **Receive the updated bank details** from the claimant (provided by email). 
2. **Verify the details** (account number, sort code, and name) against the policy holder’s (Halstead Joinery Limited) details on file to ensure accuracy. 
3. **Log the change** in the claim file using the internal “Change Payee” form (TM‑CL‑CHG‑01) via the claims portal. 
4. **Submit a supporting document** (e‑mail copy or scanned receipt) confirming the claimant’s authority to receive payments via the new account. 
5. **Update the payment instruction** with the Insolvency & Collections team (email: collections@thornfield.co.uk) with the following fields:
   - Claim Reference: TM‑2026‑0061
   - New Payee Name: Halstead Joinery Limited
   - New Bank Account: [Insert Account Number & Sort Code as received from claimant]
   - Payment Reference: “Interim” (or as instructed)
   - Amount: GBP 50,000 (next interim payment)
6. **Request confirmation** from the Collections team (within 2 business days) that the payment will be processed under the new details.
7. **Record the confirmation receipt** (email thread or reference number) in the claim file for audit purposes.

**2. What to record on the file**
- **Date of receipt**: [Insert date claimant sent the email] (e.g., 23‑09‑2026).
- **New bank account details**:
  - Account Number:
  - Sort Code:
  - Account Name (as per bank statement):
- **Claimant signature / authorisation**: copy of the claimant’s signed email or a scanned copy of their bank‑approved authorisation letter (if available).
- **Reference to payment instruction**: Include the Collections team’s confirmation number (e.g., COL‑026‑001).
- **Status update**: Note “Payee change approved” and the date of next interim payment scheduled (typically 14 days after confirmation).
- **Retention**: Store the email and supporting document in the case file under the same claim reference for future reference.

**3. Follow‑up actions**
- **Within 5 working days** of confirmation, update the Reserve Account balance to reflect the interim payment schedule.
- **Notify the Large Loss Unit** if any discrepancies arise (e.g., delayed confirmation or mismatched account details).

By adhering to these steps, the claim remains compliant with policy requirements and maintains a clear audit trail for the next interim payment.
