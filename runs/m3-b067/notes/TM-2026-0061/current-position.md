# Case Note: Current Position and Outstanding Actions
**Claim:** TM-2026-0061  
**Policyholder:** Halstead Joinery Limited  
**Policy:** TM-CP-118460  
**Date prepared:** 22 September 2026

## Current Position

This is a large loss claim arising from a fire in the insured's dust extraction plant on 2 April 2026. The cause has been established as a failed bearing that overheated and ignited accumulated dust. Cover is in principle confirmed.

**Reserve:** £214,000 (material damage £160,000, business interruption £40,000, fees £14,000)  
**Paid to date:** £60,000 (interim payment for material damage)  
**Outstanding reserve:** £154,000

### Material Damage
Damage to the extraction plant, adjoining finishing shop and roof. Interim payment of £60,000 released in April 2026 to fund replacement plant. Adjuster appointed and forensic investigator has completed their report. No issues identified with the hot work warranty (records confirmed complete).

### Business Interruption
Indemnity period: twelve months. Element under review with forensic accountant as at 30 April. Insured has moved finishing work to a subcontractor (authorised increased cost of working, reduces gross profit loss).

### Recovery
On 2 June 2026, recovery was considered against the maintenance contractor for the extraction plant. Service records showed the last inspection was eleven months before the fire, against a contractual interval of six months. Matter referred to the recovery team.

## Outstanding Actions

1. **Business interruption settlement** – Await conclusion of forensic accountant's review and agreed final quantum
2. **Recovery against maintenance contractor** – Follow up position with recovery team
3. **Final adjuster settlement** – Agree material damage settlement with loss adjuster once all elements resolved
